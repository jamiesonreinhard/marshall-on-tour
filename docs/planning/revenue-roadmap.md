# Revenue Roadmap: $1,000-2,000/month in 6 Months

**Timeline:** 6 months from launch  
**Primary Revenue:** Affiliate commissions (Amazon Associates, Booking.com)

---

## The Math

### Revenue Breakdown

**Option 1: Tennis Gear Focus (Amazon Associates)**
- Average racket: $200-250
- Commission: 4.5% = $9-11 per sale
- To hit $1,000/month: **90-110 racket sales**
- To hit $2,000/month: **180-220 racket sales**

**Option 2: Travel Focus (Booking.com)**
- Average hotel booking: $1,200/week
- Commission: 25-40% = $300-480 per booking
- To hit $1,000/month: **2-3 hotel bookings**
- To hit $2,000/month: **4-6 hotel bookings**

**Option 3: Mixed Strategy (Recommended)**
- 50% gear sales (45-55 rackets = $450-600)
- 50% travel bookings (1-2 bookings = $400-600)
- **Total: $850-1,200/month**

## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Traffic Doesn't Grow
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on social/community early
- Paid ads if needed (test ROI)
- Guest posting for backlinks
- SEO optimization from day 1

### Risk 2: Low Conversion Rates
**Mitigation:**
- A/B test affiliate link placement
- Focus on high-intent content
- Optimize for travel (higher commissions)
- Clear CTAs and value propositions

### Risk 3: Content Quality Issues
**Mitigation:**
- Use fact-checker/editor pipeline
- Real data grounding (prevents AI flagging)
- Authentic voice (Marshall's personality)
- Regular quality reviews

### Risk 4: Affiliate Account Issues
**Mitigation:**
- Set up accounts early (Month 1)
- Diversify (Amazon + Booking.com)
- Follow affiliate program rules
- Track everything
