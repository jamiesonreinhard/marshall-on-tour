# Tennis Gear Affiliate Strategy for Marshall

## The Challenge
Most major tennis brands (Head, Wilson, Babolat, Yonex) **don't have direct affiliate programs**. They sell primarily through:
- Authorized retailers (Tennis Warehouse, Tennis Express, etc.)
- Their own websites
- Big box stores (Dick's, Academy Sports, etc.)

## Best Affiliate Options

### 1. **Amazon Associates** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
**Why it's the best:**
- **4-8% commission** on sports equipment (varies by category)
- **Huge selection** - all major brands available
- **Easy to join** - free, quick approval
- **Reliable tracking** - 24-hour cookie window
- **High conversion** - people trust Amazon
- **One program** - covers Head, Wilson, Babolat, Yonex, etc.

**Commission Rates:**
- Sports & Outdoors: 4.5% (first $10k), then 4.0%
- Electronics (if selling smart rackets/tech): 4.0%

**Strategy:**
- Link to specific rackets: "Wilson Blade 98 v9" → Amazon product page
- Link to gear bundles: "Complete Tennis Starter Kit" → Amazon list
- Use Amazon's link builder for easy tracking

**Example Links:**
- `https://amazon.com/dp/B08XYZ123?tag=marshallontour-20`
- Can create "a.co" short links for cleaner URLs

---

### 2. **Tennis Warehouse Affiliate Program**
**Why it's good:**
- **5-7% commission** (varies by product category)
- **Tennis-specific** - better selection than Amazon for niche gear
- **Expert reviews** - they have detailed racket specs
- **Higher ticket items** - premium rackets, stringing machines

**Commission Rates:**
- Rackets: ~5%
- Apparel: ~7%
- Accessories: ~6%

**How to Join:**
- Contact their affiliate team directly
- Usually requires some traffic/engagement proof
- May have minimum sales requirements

**Best For:**
- Deep gear reviews
- Technical comparisons
- High-end equipment

---

### 3. **Tennis Express Affiliate Program**
**Similar to Tennis Warehouse:**
- **4-6% commission**
- Tennis-specific retailer
- Good for rackets, strings, shoes

**How to Join:**
- Contact via their website
- May require traffic proof

---

### 4. **ShareASale / Commission Junction**
**Why consider:**
- **Aggregator platforms** - multiple brands in one place
- Some smaller tennis brands may be listed
- **3-10% commission** (varies widely)

**Downside:**
- Most major brands (Head, Wilson, Babolat) still not available
- More complex to manage multiple programs
