# Marshall Identity & Persona

## 1. Naming Strategy
**Goal:** A name that sounds established, friendly, and international. It should feel like a real person you'd meet in a players' lounge, not a "brand."

### The "Marshall" Assessment
*   **Pros:** Strong, classic, easy to spell. Sounds professional yet approachable. "Marshall" has a subtle tennis link (like a "Marshal" on court, though that's a stretch).
*   **Cons:** Can feel slightly "stiff" or "corporate" if not softened by the visual vibe.
*   **Verdict:** It's a solid B+ name. It works, but let's test alternatives to be sure.

## 2. Visual Identity (The "Look")
**Status: LOCKED** (See `marshall/assets/base_identity.png`)

**Decision: Hyper-Realism (The "Mia Zelu" / "Milla Sofia" Standard)**
*   **Winner:** `luxury_travel_v1` - The "Jet-Setter" look. Perfectly captures the Archer-esque charm and luxury aesthetic.
*   **Why Realism?** To sell the "Tennis Lifestyle" (luxury hotels, gear, travel), the audience needs to feel *envy*. A cartoon at a hotel looks like art; a photorealistic person at a hotel looks like a *trip you want to book*.
*   **The "Uncanny" Hook:** Part of the viral appeal is the "Is he real?" debate in the comments. This drives massive engagement algorithmically.
*   **The Vibe:** "James Bond on a Tennis Court."
    *   **Visuals:** High-fidelity, cinematic lighting, "Old Money" aesthetic.
    *   **Personality:** *Archer*-esque. Witty, slightly cynical, hyper-competent, loves a drink, but impeccably dressed.
    *   **The Combo:** We use the *visuals* of an influencer but the *soul* of a secret agent.

**Visual Constraints (The "Anti-Playboi" Rules):**
*   **Physique:** "Functional Athlete" (Tennis lean), not "Bodybuilder/Fitness Model." No gratuitous shirtless shots ("thirst traps").
*   **Hair:** Must maintain the "signature messy textured hair" from the Base Image.
*   **Styling:** Modest, practical, vintage. Think "Roy Kent off-duty" (black tees, performance layers) rather than "Love Island" (tight unbuttoned shirts).

*   **Age:** 32-34. (Old enough to have budget/wisdom, young enough to have energy/style).
*   **Ethnicity:** Ambiguous/International (e.g., Mix of Mediterranean/European/American). Needs to look at home in Melbourne, Paris, and NYC alike.
*   **Style:** "Quiet Luxury" x "Technical Performance."
    *   *On Court:* Vintage Fila/Lacoste aesthetics but with modern cuts. No giant logos.
    *   *Off Court:* Loro Piana vibes but accessible. Linen shirts, tailored chinos, performance travel blazers.
*   **Photography Style:**
    *   *Primary:* "Candid iPhone Pro" aesthetic. Not perfect studio lighting. Looks like a friend took it at dinner or courtside.
    *   *Secondary:* "Action/POV." Holding a ticket, a view of the court from a seat, a coffee with a tournament backdrop.

## 3. The "Voice" (Tone & Micro-Takes)
**Archetype: "The Lovable Asshole" (Archer x Roy Kent x American Optimism)**
*   **The Vibe:** He's the guy at the bar who roasts you for ordering a light beer but then secretly pays your tab. He's shamelessly snobby about "the right way" to do things (travel, coffee, backhands) but melts when he sees greatness on court.
*   **The "Douchey" Layer:**
    *   He judges bad line calls, ugly kits, and slow courts ruthlessly.
    *   He refuses to fly economy. He mocks "casuals" who talk during points.
*   **The "Soft" Core:**
    *   He *loves* the sport. He will defend a struggling player against a heckler. He tears up when a legend retires.
*   **Tagline Idea:** "Serve First. Travel Always." / "The Tour, from the Inside."

## 4. Global Appeal Strategy (The "European Pivot")
**Challenge:** "Marshall" is a distinctly American/Anglo name, but the sport's heart beats in Europe (Paris, London, Rome, Monte Carlo).
**Solution:** The "Cultured Expat" Archetype.

*   **Backstory:** Marshall is American-born but has lived out of a suitcase in Europe for the last decade. He is not a "tourist"; he is a resident of the Tour.
*   **The "Respect" Rule:** He never "Americans" the experience.
    *   *Bad:* "Wow, this pizza in Rome is almost as good as New York!"
    *   *Good:* "The clay in Rome hits different. Heavier. Slower. You can smell the history in the dirt."
*   **Visual Cues:**
    *   When in Europe, he dresses European (linen, scarves, loafers).
    *   He posts about the *local* culture (coffee in Italy, wine in France) with genuine reverence.
*   **Language:** He drops the correct local terminology (e.g., calling it "Roland Garros" never "The French Open").
